A complete 3D flight game: fly the Meridian Kestrel around Pulau Meridian — thread 8 rings, land on Runway Meridian, then race the gold ring by Gunung Meridian before the timer dies. Throttle, roll, pitch and yaw on the keyboard; chase or cockpit camera; fuel and damage turn every sortie into decisions.

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome (or serve the `game/` folder with any static server). No network, no build step.

## Controls

W/S throttle · A/D roll · Arrow keys pitch · Q/E yaw · Space brake · Shift boost · C camera · R restart · M mute

## Missions

1. **Ring Run** — fly through all 8 orange rings (+250 each)
2. **Landing** — land on Runway Meridian under 110 kt and under 25 m (+1000, fuel refilled)
3. **Timed Delivery** — reach the gold ring by the north peak before the timer runs out (+1500)
